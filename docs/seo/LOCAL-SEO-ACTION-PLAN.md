# Local SEO action plan

All items here need owner access to external accounts; none were done from
the codebase.

## NAP — one version everywhere

Use exactly what the site publishes (Settings → contacts/address):

- **Name**: Stella Sharm Tours
- **Address**: 152 Malek El Bahrain Street, Old Market, Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt
- **Phone**: the number in Settings (shown on the contact page)
- **Website**: https://stellasharmtours.com/ (use `/it` for Italian listings)
- **Map pin**: the office pin used on the contact page (27.8669735, 34.293776)

Before listing: confirm the legal entity ("by Heroes Travel — to confirm" in
`docs/DECISIONS.md`) and whether a tourism licence number may be shown.

## Profiles

| Profile                 | Action                                                                                                                                                           | Notes                                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Google Business Profile | Claim/verify; primary category "Tour agency" (or "Travel agency"); hours, photos of the office and real trips, products/services = collections; link to the site | Biggest local lever (Maps, local pack) |
| Bing Places             | Import from Google Business Profile                                                                                                                              | Also feeds Copilot                     |
| Apple Business Connect  | Claim the place card                                                                                                                                             | Apple Maps / Siri                      |
| Tripadvisor             | Claim the attraction/operator listing                                                                                                                            | Strong for travel queries in UK and IT |
| Facebook / Instagram    | Already linked; keep NAP identical                                                                                                                               | Listed in `sameAs`                     |

When a profile URL exists, add it in Admin → Settings → social links; it then
appears in `sameAs` automatically.

## Reviews — genuine only

- After a completed trip, staff send the Google review link by WhatsApp (one
  message, no incentive, no filtering of unhappy customers).
- Reply to every review, in the reviewer's language.
- Do not copy reviews onto the site with review markup; if reviews are shown,
  use an official widget/link and keep them visible and attributable.

## Partnerships and links (legitimate)

- Hotels in Naama Bay / Nabq / Sharks Bay: concierge pages or printed QR to
  the site (no paid link schemes).
- Italian travel communities (forums, Facebook groups): answer questions as
  the company, disclose who you are, link only where helpful.
- Travel bloggers who actually take a trip: they choose whether to link.
- Local tourism directories with editorial review.

No fake listings, fake addresses, purchased reviews or link networks.
