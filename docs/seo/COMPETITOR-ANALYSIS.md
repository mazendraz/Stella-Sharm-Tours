# Competitor and search-intent analysis

## Method and limits (read first)

- **51 queries** in total: 3 on 2026-11-27, 18 on 2026-11-28 (10 English,
  8 Italian) and **30 more on 2026-11-28 in the phase-3 completion session
  (#22–51: 17 English, 13 Italian; 7 of them conversational, 1 local)**.
  Tool: a web-search API located in the US. It is **not** Google Italy or
  Google UK, has no personalisation, and returns a result list plus a machine
  summary, not Google ranking positions.
- Statements such as "a Sinai-only stamp does not allow Cairo" are what
  competitor listings, forums and Q&A sites **say**. They are not verified and
  must not be published by Stella without an official, dated source.
- "Order" below is the order in that tool's result list. It is evidence of
  **who is visible for the topic**, not a Google position. Positions in Italy
  and the UK must be measured with Search Console (our own site) and a rank
  tracker or manual incognito checks from IT/UK (competitors).
- No keyword volumes, difficulty, backlinks or traffic: no such tool was
  available. Nothing in this file estimates them.
- Prices quoted are what the listings showed on the day, in the listing's
  currency. They are **observations, not facts about the market**, and they
  were not used for Stella's prices (prices come only from the database).
- The 50-query target is now met (51). Monthly re-checks of the search part
  live in `AI-VISIBILITY-BENCHMARK.md` and are recorded in Admin → AI
  visibility.

## What the result pages answer that Stella's pages don't (observed)

1. **Park, marina and safari fees** — almost every Ras Mohammed, Tiran, Blue
   Hole and desert listing says whether the fee is included and how much is
   paid in cash (observed: $5–$20 park fees, ~€10 safari fee). Stella states
   "Marina fees" included on one trip and nothing elsewhere.
2. **Guide language** — Italian pages lead with "guida italiana" (DLT,
   Alpitour, Civitatis). Stella cannot say this until it is confirmed.
3. **Duration and night departures in plain words** — Sinai 12–17 h, Cairo by
   bus ~22 h with 6–8 h each way. Stella shows departure times; the duration
   and the night-bus reality need one plain sentence.
4. **Documents** — passport/ID for boats; one listing warns that a
   **Sinai-only entry stamp does not allow travel to Cairo**. This is a real,
   high-stakes question for Cairo/Luxor buyers (needs an official source).
5. **Cancellation terms** next to the price ("free cancellation up to 24 h").
   Stella's cancellation page is still pending.
6. **Health/suitability** (pregnancy, back problems, fitness for Sinai).
7. **Comparisons** — Alpitour's "Cairo in one day: plane or bus" guide is
   exactly the kind of page that wins comparison queries.

Added from queries 22–51:

8. **Informational results are owned by media, not operators** (#22, #23,
   #40): Rough Guides, Culture Trip, lastminute, Alpitour editorial. A local
   operator competes there only with genuinely first-hand guides.
9. **Conversational questions get affiliate, forum or Q&A pages** (#38, #40,
   #45, #47, #51). They answer in two lines; none answers with an operator's
   own published facts. This is the AEO gap the re-verified FAQ answers
   (`data/content/faq-verified.json`) and the guide drafts target.
10. **Ras Mohammed by land vs by boat confuses searchers** (#29, #30, #38): one
    summary claimed no land tour exists while others list half-day bus tours.
    Stella runs boat, bus and private car; FAQ #44 now explains the difference
    from Stella's own catalogue.
11. **Italian-speaking guide** is what Italian operators lead with (#39, #48:
    Alpitour, DLT, sharm-club.com, Buongiorno Egitto). Still blocked for
    Stella until the owner confirms guide languages (decision D2).
12. **WhatsApp booking is common** (#26, #44), so "book on WhatsApp" alone is
    not a differentiator; "request first, no online payment" plus private-first
    is.
13. **Programmatic per-hotel pages** (Egyptra, up to 15 languages, #50) are a
    tactic Stella should **not** copy: near-duplicate thin pages are exactly
    what the publication rules block.
14. **Families** (#37): FamilyGO owns the Italian family query with a real
    guide; Stella's family guide draft needs first-hand detail to compete.
15. **Fees in cash on the spot** recur again (#30, #32, #45: park fee, ~€10
    safari tax, Blue Hole tickets) — still the most common gap on Stella's
    trip pages, blocked by owner confirmation.
