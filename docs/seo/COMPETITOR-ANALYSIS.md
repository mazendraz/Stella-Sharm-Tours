# Competitor and search-intent analysis

## Method and limits (read first)

- **21 queries** in total: 3 on 2026-11-27 and 18 on 2026-11-28 (10 English,
  8 Italian). Tool: a web-search API located in the US. It is **not** Google
  Italy or Google UK, has no personalisation, and returns a result list, not
  Google ranking positions.
- "Order" below is the order in that tool's result list. It is evidence of
  **who is visible for the topic**, not a Google position. Positions in Italy
  and the UK must be measured with Search Console (our own site) and a rank
  tracker or manual incognito checks from IT/UK (competitors).
- No keyword volumes, difficulty, backlinks or traffic: no such tool was
  available. Nothing in this file estimates them.
- Prices quoted are what the listings showed on the day, in the listing's
  currency. They are **observations, not facts about the market**, and they
  were not used for Stella's prices (prices come only from the database).
- The target of 50 queries was not reached. The remaining 29 queries are in
  `AI-VISIBILITY-BENCHMARK.md` (search part) to be run monthly.

## What the result pages answer that Stella's pages don't (observed)

1. **Park, marina and safari fees** — almost every Ras Mohammed, Tiran, Blue
   Hole and desert listing says whether the fee is included and how much is
   paid in cash (observed: $5–$20 park fees, ~€10 safari fee). Stella states
   "Marina fees" included on one trip and nothing elsewhere.
2. **Guide language** — Italian pages lead with "guida italiana" (DLT,
   Alpitour, Civitatis). Stella cannot say this until it is confirmed.
3. **Duration and night departures in plain words** — Sinai 12–17 h, Cairo by
   bus ~22 h with 6–8 h each way. Stella shows departure times; the duration
   and the night-bus reality need one plain sentence.
4. **Documents** — passport/ID for boats; one listing warns that a
   **Sinai-only entry stamp does not allow travel to Cairo**. This is a real,
   high-stakes question for Cairo/Luxor buyers (needs an official source).
5. **Cancellation terms** next to the price ("free cancellation up to 24 h").
   Stella's cancellation page is still pending.
6. **Health/suitability** (pregnancy, back problems, fitness for Sinai).
7. **Comparisons** — Alpitour's "Cairo in one day: plane or bus" guide is
   exactly the kind of page that wins comparison queries.
