# FAQ candidates (draft — not published)

60 questions travellers ask about excursions from Sharm El Sheikh, collected
from the 18 searches in `COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS.md` (EN and IT result pages,
competitor FAQ and "what's included" sections) and from the gaps in
`TOUR-VERIFICATION-CHECKLIST.md`.

**Status**

- **Site** — answerable today from how the website works or from published
  catalogue data; the draft answer below can be published after the owner
  reads it.
- **Owner** — needs a business fact the owner must confirm. No answer is
  drafted, because writing one would mean guessing.
- **Legal** — needs the approved terms/cancellation policy.

**Where it goes** — the page the answer is most useful on. Each question has
one home; the general FAQ page only gets questions that apply to every trip,
so the same answer is not repeated across pages.

Answers must be short, direct and true on their own (they are quoted by search
and AI answers out of context). Publish in all seven languages together.

## Publishing rules

- Publish an answer only after the owner has read it in all seven languages.
- One home per question (avoid identical FAQs on many pages). Trip-specific
  answers belong in the trip's notes, exclusions or "what to bring" fields,
  which already render on the page and in structured data.
- FAQ rich results are not shown for this kind of site; the value is clear
  answers for travellers and for AI answers, not a SERP feature.
